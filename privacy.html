Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

McBride Apartments is a private application used only by the owners of a six-unit apartment building in McBride, British Columbia (Mile 90 Investments Ltd.). It is not available to the public.

What the app accesses

With the owners' permission, the app connects to the building's own Google account and uses two Gmail permissions:

  • Read-only access (gmail.readonly) to read the building's mail, such as rent payment notifications and messages from tenants. It cannot send, change or delete mail.
  • Insert access (gmail.insert) to place notification messages for the owners into that same inbox. It cannot send email to any other address.

How the information is used and stored

Information read from Gmail is used only to help the owners manage the building: recording rent payments, tracking leases, and preparing reply drafts for the owners to review. It is stored only on the owners' own computer.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising. It does not send email to anyone on its own; any reply to another person is reviewed and sent by the owners themselves.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account under Third-party apps & services. Data stored on the owners' computer can be deleted by the owners at any time.
